About Me

I'm an information security engineer with five-plus years of experience spanning the Security Management Team and CERT at Koscom, where I work on threat detection, incident response, and internal security tooling for financial-sector infrastructure. I hold a CISSP certification and a B.S. in Information Systems from Hanyang University, with an exchange semester at the University of Konstanz, Germany.

Lately, most of my time goes into building AI-CERT, an on-premises LLM-powered SOC automation system, and preparing to pursue a Master's degree focused on security research.
